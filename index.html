Our Story

Your Friendly Neighborhood Dhaba in Mohansarai

Welcome to Swad Thikana Dhaba. Experience the true essence of a classic Indian dhaba right here in Varanasi. We serve piping hot, authentic desi food cooked with traditional spices and a whole lot of love.

Enjoy our robust North Indian curries, fresh tandoori rotis, and classic dhaba-style dal makhani in a warm, rustic setting. At Swad Thikana, every meal is a homestyle feast.
